# Draft Request for Blockscout Commercial Licensing Terms

Status: Draft for the operator's review. Not sent. No license, payment, or other commitment has been accepted.

Suggested subject: ColossusX — commercial use, public source distribution, and attribution permissions

To the Blockscout licensing team,

We operate ColossusX and are preparing a public GitHub release. We want to permit commercial use and redistribution of that release under an appropriate open-source license, while obtaining any additional rights required from Blockscout through a written commercial agreement.

Our current components are:

- Deployed backend: Blockscout v9.0.2, commit `ed93c12dc1835ec0e7fb3e4be3504ceb7061610e`, distributed with GPLv3.
- Customized frontend: Blockscout frontend v2.3.0, distributed with GPLv3, with branding, footer, asset, and statistics-display modifications.
- Statistics service: stats-v2.9.2, commit `bbf2d71618e39d61edb2ada7ad7ab19cf06ae586`.
- Development checkout also under consideration: backend 11.3.1, commit `b7671f6cef934eb6940c20bc8c0be336fee45f9c`, carrying the Blockscout Software Licence effective 2026-04-22.

The frontend currently uses the ColossusX identity. Its original Blockscout footer attribution, displayed copyright line, and source/version links were removed. The upstream GPL license file remains in the runtime image. We are reviewing the notice and corresponding-source requirements and do not assume that a commercial license has already been granted.

Please confirm the availability, pricing, and written scope of an agreement addressing:

1. Which exact versions, repositories, copyright interests, and future upgrades the agreement covers, and which third-party components or rights it excludes.
2. Permission to publish and distribute modified source code and compiled releases on public GitHub, including whether we may release covered modifications under GPLv3 or another mutually agreed OSI-approved license.
3. The rights of downstream recipients to use, modify, redistribute, and commercially use that public release, including whether each recipient would require a separate agreement. We need to understand whether the proposed terms can support a genuine open-source distribution.
4. Permission for paid hosting, API access, support, customization, and other intended commercial services, with any limits stated explicitly.
5. The permitted use of the ColossusX product name and mascot, whether removing Blockscout product branding is authorized, and the exact copyright, attribution, license, warranty, source-code, and trademark notices that must remain visible or accompany distributions.
6. Whether a compact legal-notices link can satisfy the permitted interface presentation, and any required wording, placement, links, or artwork.
7. Whether any consent concerning earlier notice changes is available, and any remediation you require. We do not assume that a future agreement automatically covers earlier conduct.
8. Ownership and licensing of our original changes, any sublicensing or dual-licensing permission, and whether contractual rights are compatible with the intended downstream open-source grant.
9. Fees, duration, termination provisions, post-termination distribution rights, renewal terms, and any reporting requirements.

Please provide the proposed agreement and any required notice wording for review before we make a public release or rely on additional commercial permissions.

Operator legal entity, contact details, and the intended public repository URL: to be supplied by the operator before sending.
