# Browser favicon composition

Updated 2026-09-21 using the built-in imagegen tool. The supplied
`logo-512.png` was the reference/edit target. Page and mobile-header artwork
retains the original full-body image; browser favicon variants use
`favicon-master.png` so the recognizable head occupies more of the fixed tab
icon area. The Apple touch icon is unchanged. Custom artwork remains CC0-1.0.

Prompt: Edit the exact supplied CypherTrooper mascot into a square, tight
upper-body favicon master. Preserve the black and white 3D mascot, original
curved emblem, texture, waving hand and CYPHERTROOPER chest tag. Center the
round head at approximately 75-80 percent of the width, with minimal white
margin. Lower legs and feet may be outside the crop. Keep the original white
background. No redesign, new lettering, border, Wrapped CPH or added graphics.

The existing favicons 7.x tooling packages this master into 16, 32, 48 and
192 pixel PNGs and an ICO. Output hashes are recorded in `sources.json`.
